Why Radova Linux?

An operating system that knows when to step aside.

Most modern distributions ship with hundreds of background daemons, silent telemetry, and heavy shells that drain your resources before you even open a single window.

Radova Linux takes a quieter path. Engineered for sustained peak performance without the background noise, it pairs a pure Arch Linux foundation with a clean, cohesive desktop right out of the box — keeping your machine cool, responsive, and effortless on both vintage laptops and modern workstations.

Featherlight Footprint

Zero Telemetry, Ever

Absolute privacy by default. No background analytics, no usage tracking, and no silent data collection.
